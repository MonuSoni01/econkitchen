PROJECT OVERVIEW

The brief was simple:
more storage, less clutter.

The homeowners wanted a kitchen that felt clean, practical and easy to maintain, without making the room visually heavy.

The existing layout had underused corners, limited drawer storage and an unplanned appliance zone. The project focused on improving workflow while keeping the design calm.
