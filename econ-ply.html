MATERIAL KNOWLEDGE

Choose materials by application, not only by name.

The right board depends on where it will be used, how much moisture it may face, the type of finish planned and the expected service life.

Moisture Exposure

Wet, humid and dry areas can require different plywood or board specifications. Material selection should match the environment.

Core & Construction

Core structure, density and manufacturing quality influence panel stability, strength and long-term performance.

Surface & Finish

Laminate, veneer, paint and other finishes can place different demands on the base material and its surface consistency.

Thickness & Use

Thickness should be selected according to span, load, construction detail and application requirement.
