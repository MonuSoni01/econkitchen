Aapke Common Questions

Kya carpenter branded hardware use kar sakta hai?

Haan. Skilled carpenter branded hinges, drawers aur accessories use kar sakta hai. Product compatibility, correct fitting aur installation instructions follow karna zaroori hai.

Kya factory-finished hamesha mehenga hota hai?

Zaroori nahi. Total cost kitchen size, material, finish, hardware aur included work par depend karti hai. Dono quotations ko same specifications aur scope par compare karein.

Kya existing platform retain kar sakte hain?

Kai projects mein possible hota hai. Platform ki condition, measurements, plumbing aur proposed cabinet layout check karne ke baad suitability confirm ki ja sakti hai.
