Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in the ₹5,000 DIY Kitchen Blueprint?

Site measurement / measurement guidance, 2D layout, 3D design, material specifications, finish selection, hardware and accessory planning, detailed estimate and final quotation format.

How long does it take to get the plan?

Share your kitchen details on WhatsApp. Our team will confirm the delivery timeline based on your requirements and the measurements available.

Can I use the plan with my own carpenter or interior designer?

Yes. Your Kitchen. Your Plan. Your Choice. You can use the Blueprint with your own carpenter, contractor or interior designer.

What if I later want to order from ECON Kitchen?

Your Blueprint can be converted into an ECON Kitchen production quotation. A ₹10,000 adjustment is available on eligible ECON Kitchen orders, as per applicable terms.

Do you provide site visits?

Site measurement / measurement guidance is part of the planning package. Share your location so our team can confirm the online or on-site arrangements available for your project.

Is this suitable for NRIs?

Yes. You can share your floor plan, available measurements, site photos and requirements to start planning your home or family kitchen project.

Is quotation compare really free?

Yes. Send your existing kitchen quotation on WhatsApp for the FREE Quotation Check. The ₹5,000 DIY Kitchen Blueprint is a separate planning package.

What details do you need from me?

Share your quotation if available, kitchen shape, size or measurements, 2–4 site photos, floor plan, preferred style, appliances, storage requirements and approximate budget.
