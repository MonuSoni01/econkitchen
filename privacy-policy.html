01

Introduction

ECON Kitchen respects the privacy of visitors, customers and individuals who communicate with us through our website and other business communication channels.

This Privacy Policy describes the types of information that may be collected when you interact with ECON Kitchen and explains how that information may be used, stored and protected.

By using this website or voluntarily submitting information through our forms, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

02

Information We May Collect

The information collected depends on how you interact with our website and services.

Information You Provide

When you submit an enquiry, request a consultation, ask for a quotation or otherwise contact us, you may provide information such as:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • City or location
  • Interior service requirements
  • Property or project-related details that you choose to share
  • Messages, feedback or other information submitted to us

Website & Technical Information

Depending on the technologies enabled on our website, certain technical information may also be collected automatically, such as browser type, device type, pages visited, referral information and general website interaction data.

03

How We Use Your Information

Information collected through the website may be used for legitimate business and customer-service purposes, including:

  • Responding to enquiries and consultation requests
  • Understanding your interior design requirements
  • Preparing quotations or project discussions
  • Contacting you regarding requested services
  • Providing customer support
  • Managing leads and customer interactions
  • Improving our website, services and customer experience
  • Understanding website performance and visitor engagement
  • Protecting our website and business systems against misuse or security threats
  • Meeting applicable legal or regulatory requirements
04

Calls, Email & WhatsApp Communication

If you submit your contact information or contact ECON Kitchen directly, our team may respond using the communication details provided by you.

Depending on your enquiry and communication preferences, this may include communication through phone calls, email, WhatsApp or other appropriate business channels.

Such communication may relate to your enquiry, consultation, quotation, project requirement, service updates or relevant follow-up.

You can ask our team to stop non-essential promotional communication through the same communication channel.

05

Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, remember preferences, understand visitor behaviour and improve website performance.

Analytics and advertising technologies may also be used where configured on the website. These technologies may collect information about interactions with the website, device characteristics and general browsing activity.

You can manage or block cookies using your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

06

How Information May Be Shared

ECON Kitchen does not sell personal information simply for monetary consideration.

Information may, however, be shared where reasonably necessary with service providers or technology partners that support our website and business operations.

These may include providers supporting:

  • Website hosting
  • Website analytics
  • Customer communication
  • Lead and enquiry management
  • Advertising and campaign measurement
  • Technical and website support

Information may also be disclosed where required by law, regulation, legal process or a valid request from an authorised authority.

07

Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no internet-based website, electronic communication or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should therefore avoid submitting unnecessary confidential or sensitive information through general website forms.

08

Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to an enquiry, manage a customer relationship, provide requested services, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes and meet applicable legal obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the nature of the information and the purpose for which it was collected.

09

Your Choices & Requests

Subject to applicable law and the nature of your relationship with us, you may contact ECON Kitchen regarding personal information that you have provided to us.

You may request assistance concerning:

  • Updating inaccurate contact information
  • Correcting information you previously submitted
  • Stopping non-essential promotional communication
  • Questions about how information provided by you is being handled
  • Other privacy-related requests available under applicable law

We may need to verify the request before taking certain actions to help protect customer information from unauthorised access or changes.

10

Third-Party Websites & Services

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, social media platforms, maps, messaging services or other external services.

Once you access an external service, its own privacy policy and terms may apply. ECON Kitchen is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of independent third-party websites and services.

11

Children's Privacy

ECON Kitchen's website and interior services are intended for customers and individuals seeking home interior-related information and services. Our website is not designed specifically to collect personal information from children.

12

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website, services, technologies or applicable requirements.

When the policy is updated, the revised version may be published on this page with an updated revision date.
