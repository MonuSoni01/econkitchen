WHERE IT STARTED

Before ECON Kitchen, there was ECON Ply.

ECON's foundation was built around plywood, materials and the realities of furniture and interior work.

Years of dealing with boards, finishes, applications, durability, sourcing and customer requirements created a practical understanding of what goes inside a good interior product.

That experience later became a strong base for our kitchen journey — where materials are not just sold, but selected, engineered and applied inside complete modular kitchens.

Material Knowledge Understanding boards, finishes and applications.
Manufacturing Mindset Thinking beyond retail into production and consistency.
Kitchen Application Turning material experience into complete kitchen solutions.
