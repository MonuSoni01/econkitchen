Frequently Asked Questions About Modular Kitchens

A modular kitchen is made using planned cabinet modules designed according to your kitchen space, storage needs and preferred style. At ECON Kitchen, every kitchen is customised for your home.

The right layout depends on your available space, kitchen dimensions and daily usage. Common options include L-shaped, U-shaped, straight, parallel and island kitchens. Our team can help you choose the right layout for your space.

We offer material options such as BWP plywood and HDHMR, along with different finishes, hardware and accessories. The right combination can be selected according to your kitchen requirements and preferences.

The cost of a modular kitchen depends on its size, layout, materials, finishes, hardware and accessories. Share your kitchen measurements or visit our showroom for a detailed estimate based on your requirements.

The timeline depends on the kitchen design, materials, finishes and site conditions. Once the design and specifications are finalised, our team will share the expected manufacturing and installation timeline.
