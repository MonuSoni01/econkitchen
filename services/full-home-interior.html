HAVE QUESTIONS?

Everything you need to know.

What does a complete interior project include?

The agreed scope can include kitchen, wardrobes, living and bedroom furniture, doors, mandir, TV units, PVC panels and CNC detailing.

Can I choose just one room or service?

Yes. Share the room or service you need and we can plan a suitable scope.

Are the designs and finishes customisable?

Dimensions, storage, colours and suitable finishes can be selected during consultation.

How are cost and delivery time decided?

They depend on measurements, material selection, project scope and site conditions. Your quotation confirms the agreed details.
